# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in the Axon project a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background
or identity.

## Our standards

We expect everyone to:

- Be kind, patient, and respectful in all interactions.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Behavior that harms, harasses, demeans, or excludes others is not tolerated, in project
spaces or in public when representing the project.

## Our standard, in full

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version
2.1, as its full Code of Conduct. Please read the canonical text — including the detailed
behavior standards, enforcement guidelines, and scope — here:

> https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Enforcement & reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it confidentially to the project
maintainer:

**Daniel Tamas — hello@danieltamas.ro**

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated
to respect the privacy and security of anyone who reports an incident, and may take any action
they deem appropriate, up to and including a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
